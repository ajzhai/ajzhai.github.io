**About me**

I am a Ph.D. student in the computer science department at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC), advised by [Prof. Shenlong Wang](https://shenlong.web.illinois.edu/). Before coming to UIUC, I received my B.S. in computer science from Caltech.

My research is in computer vision and robotics. Broadly speaking, I develop methods that enable computers to *perceive* the current state of the world and *predict* action outcomes to guide decision-making.

I am Chinese American. My middle name, Jianqiao, is my Chinese given name (翟健乔). However, I usually go by Albert.
